Research

X-ray scattering metrology for semiconductor nanostructures

I study X-ray scattering techniques for the nondestructive characterization of nanoscale semiconductor patterns. My research focuses on critical-dimension small-angle X-ray scattering (CD-SAXS), combining scattering simulations and inverse modeling to reconstruct the cross-sectional profiles of nanoscale patterns. I also investigate metrology challenges associated with EUV lithography and advanced semiconductor fabrication.

Cross-lingual retrieval and LLM evaluation

I study retrieval-augmented language models in cross-lingual settings, where a question in one language requires evidence written in another. My research focuses on multi-hop question answering, with particular attention to retrieval effectiveness and answer faithfulness. I developed K-CrossHop, a Korean–English benchmark for examining where cross-lingual failures arise in retrieval and evidence-based answering.
